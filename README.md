# GRC205_IRP_Assignment_GCR2512877.docx
ICDFA GRC 205 ASSIGNMENT
Summary
Objective:
This project is a comprehensive incident response planning assignment for a fictional training institute, Northbridge Skills Institute, facing a suspected ransomware incident with a live examination deadline. The goal was to act as Incident Response Lead, analyse the evidence, classify the incident, and produce a coordinated, defensible response plan aligned with NIST SP 800-61r3 / CSF 2.0, CISA #StopRansomware guidance, and business continuity principles.

Achievements:

Built an evidence-linked incident timeline separating confirmed observations, hypotheses, and unresolved questions, with alternative explanations for ambiguous events.

Classified the incident as Critical using defined severity criteria and assessed CIA impact across FIN-PC07, FS01, LMS01, BAK01, and M365.

Produced an IR policy and plan with clear authority, escalation, isolation rights, documentation, and BC integration.

Defined a realistic CSIRT structure for a small IT team and created a RACI matrix covering eight core activities.

Developed a 24-hour response playbook with ordered actions, owners, approvals, evidence retention, and completion checks.

Created an evidence register with planned collection methods, integrity checks, secure storage, and custody arrangements.

Justified two difficult decisions: containment vs. evidence preservation, and restoring examination access while backup safety is uncertain.

Delivered a communication matrix, executive briefing, learner notice, and legal/privacy notification assessment distinguishing operational notices from formal breach notification.

Designed and simulated a 60-minute tabletop exercise with five injects, a decision log, success-criteria evaluation, and a measurable improvement plan.

Analysed the healthcare ransomware case study, identified strengths and limitations, and transferred two justified changes to the Northbridge plan.

All data, contacts, and incidents are fictional; evidence collection is recorded as planned or in progress, with no invented hashes or acquisitions.
